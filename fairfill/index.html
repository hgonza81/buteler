The problem

Fair value has turned compliance into copy-and-paste.

Every insurer, its own form"Each insurer sends us a different spreadsheet."

Same questions, new layout, every year — sometimes several times a year.

The same figures, again"We copy the same numbers into form after form."

Commission, cancellations, complaints, claims: pulled from your system and retyped by hand.

It eats the margin"On some lines the paperwork costs more than the commission."

Some brokers are trimming their panels just to cope with the forms.

The FCA question"Can we prove what we sent, and where the numbers came from?"

Emails and attachments scattered across inboxes are not an audit trail.

*BIBA estimates a typical broker distributing 20 products from 50 insurers faces around 1,000 fair value assessments (Health & Protection, Jan 2025). Brokers describe the work as a "cottage industry" of form-filling (Insurance Times).

Why it hasn't gone away

The tools around you weren't built for the broker's side.

Spreadsheets

Flexible, but they forget everything you answered last time.

The standard template

Helpful — when insurers use it. Many still send their own.

Fair value platforms

Designed for insurers to collect data from their distributors, not for you to answer them all.

A generic AI chat

Doesn't know your figures, and leaves no record of what was sent.

How Fairfill works

Answer once. Review in minutes. Send with a record.

  1. Load your data once

    Upload the exports you already get from your broking system. No integration project.

  2. Approve your standard answers

    Target market, services, how you're paid — written once, versioned, reused.

  3. Drop in any insurer's form

    Excel, Word or the industry template. Fairfill fills it in that insurer's own format.

  4. Review, then send

    See each answer next to its source. Nothing leaves without your approval, and every submission is logged.

Questions

What compliance teams ask us.

You're a small company. Why trust you?

Fair question. That's why the pilot is free, your data can be exported at any time, and you talk directly to the person who builds the product.

Is our data safe?

Hosted in the UK or EU, encrypted, and processed by an AI provider that does not train on your data. We sign a data processing agreement with every customer.

We already use the industry standard template.

Fairfill fills that too. The problem is the insurers who still send their own forms — Fairfill handles both from the same answers.

What if the AI gets something wrong?

Nothing is sent without your approval. Every answer shows its source, and anything uncertain is flagged and left blank for you.

One data export and one sample form. You'll see a filled questionnaire on day one.
